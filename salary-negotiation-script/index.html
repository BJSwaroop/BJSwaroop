  • Lose ₹1–3 LPA without realizing it Not from a bad offer — from a good offer they never questioned.

Offers are almost never withdrawn over a polite negotiation — hiring you already cost the company weeks of effort. What gets people in trouble is demanding, ultimatums, or lying about competing offers. The script avoids all three by design.

Then you accept graciously and you’ve lost nothing — the script includes the exact closing line for this. But “no” on base salary often still leaves room for a joining bonus, relocation support, or an early appraisal, and the PDF shows you how to pivot to those.

For freshers, a 10–20% bump on the offered CTC is a reasonable, defensible ask — the guide explains how to anchor it with market data instead of a random number. Asking for double will end the conversation; asking for nothing guarantees nothing.
